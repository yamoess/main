PROJECT BIDDING

BIDDING involves very careful study of all the available Bid Documents such as scope of the project, project drawings, project specifications, project reports (geotechnical, environmental, etc.), contract terms and conditions, etc. Once the documents are received and reviewed, a project 'work breakdown structure (WBS)' is prepared, each component of the work grouped as per the CSI format. Quantity take-off is done for each and every component - either measured or counted, in various Excel files. These quantities are then incorporated into a comprehensive and fully detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) file. This file is now ready for pricing. Each item of the project BOQ is then sorted into Subcontract items, critical packaged equipment, third party services item or other major/ minor item, etc. before converting them into Bid Packages or Request for Proposals.

Each one of the bid files contain all relevant information obtained from the client that includes but not limited to the project BOQ, Drawings, Specifications, Project Reports, Contract Terms and Conditions, Response to technical queries by the client/consultant, etc. The Bid Packages/Request for proposals are sent to designated and qualified vendors and subcontractors inviting them to quote as per the project requirements. Follow up is made with the vendors and their queries responded at earliest in coordination with the client. Once quotations are received, they are compared for completeness and compliance. Missing and other costs such as additional labor, installation equipment cost, cost of tools & consumables, safety, quality control and other costs are added to produce a Final Budget Price for each item. This file is now ready for incorporation of final mark-up and other costs that the client desires before submittal
